TCB Legal is an independent law firm based in Tunis. TCB Legal was founded with a clear objective: to provide high-level legal representation combining local expertise with an international outlook.


We advise private clients, business leaders, and companies operating in Tunisia and across borders. Through established professional partnerships in selected jurisdictions, the firm is able to assist clients involved in multi-jurisdictional matters requiring coordinated legal support.


Our structure allows us to remain agile, discreet, and personally involved in every engagement.
