TCB Legal is an independent law firm based in Tunis. TCB Legal was founded with a clear objective: to offer high-level legal representation combining local expertise and an international vision.


We advise private clients, business leaders, and companies operating in Tunisia and internationally. Through established professional partnerships in selected jurisdictions, the firm is able to assist clients involved in multi-jurisdictional matters requiring coordinated legal support across the MENA region, North Africa, and the Maghreb.


Our structure allows us to remain agile, discreet, and personally involved in every mission, while accompanying clients on both domestic and cross-border matters throughout the region.
